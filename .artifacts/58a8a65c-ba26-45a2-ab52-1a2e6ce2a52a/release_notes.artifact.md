# Release Notes - Dotz Launcher v7.4.0

## 💎 Premium Personalization

### Clock Styles - Now a PRO Feature
- **Exclusive Customization**: The new clock styles (Modern, Minimalist, Analog, and Words) are now part of the **Dotz Launcher PRO** experience.
- **Classic is Free**: The original "Classic" digital clock remains free for all users.
- **Smart Locking**: Non-PRO users will see premium badges in the settings menu. Attempting to switch styles via gestures will also respect your premium status.

## 🎨 Design & Personalization

### Gesture-Based Edit Mode
- **Instant Customization**: Long-press on the Clock or any Header Widget to enter **Edit Mode**. Just swipe left or right to cycle through all available styles instantly.
- **Unified Logic**: Swiping now reliably works across all header modes (Toggles, Stats, Weather, and Music).
- **Conflict-Free**: Smart gesture handling automatically pauses home screen paging while you are in Edit Mode to ensure smooth style cycling.

### Five New Clock Styles (PRO)
- **Classic**: The original Dotz look.
- **Modern**: Bold, high-impact typography. Automatically hides status indicators (battery/network) for a cleaner, ultra-minimalist vibe.
- **Minimalist**: Focus solely on time with an ultra-light, spaced-out font.
- **Analog**: A custom-drawn, minimalist face with dynamic hands.
- **Words**: Expresses time in calm, descriptive English (e.g., "QUARTER TO SIX").

### Atmospheric Custom Themes (PRO)
- **Deep Personalization**: Custom colors now "soak" into the launcher atmosphere. Backgrounds get a 15% tint and tiles get a 25% tint of your chosen color for a cohesive look.
- **Interactive Live Preview**: See your clock, layout, and color changes in real-time within the settings menu.

## 🌤️ Weather Refinement
- **Minimalist Layout**: Simplified the Weather header by removing the redundant time display and summary text, focusing on the essential data for a cleaner appearance.

## 🎵 Robust Music Integration
- **Hybrid Sync Engine**: Introduced a background sync loop that ensures the music header *never* misses a song change, even with "stubborn" apps like YouTube.
- **Multi-App Prioritization**: Smartly focuses on whichever app is currently playing, buffering, or was most recently active.
- **Battery Safe**: The sync engine is lifecycle-aware—it only runs when you are looking at the home screen.

## 🚀 Experience & Performance
- **Atomic Architecture**: Rebuilt the app's internal state engine from the ground up. By isolating UI updates into atomic flows, we've significantly reduced CPU overhead and improved battery efficiency.
- **Instant Startup**: Optimized the launch sequence to defer non-critical background tasks. The launcher is now interactive near-instantly upon unlock.
- **Redesigned Settings**: A faster, sectioned list replaces old expandable cards for improved navigation.
- **Fluid Navigation**: Premium slide animations for all internal screens and fixed the "wallpaper flash" bug.
- **Accessibility & Inclusivity**: Added full TalkBack support for the new Custom Color Palette and Clock styles, making Dotz more accessible to everyone.
- **Critical Fix**: Resolved an app crash related to missing media permissions during initial setup.
- **In-App Updates**: Get notified of new versions directly on your Timeline via official Google Play integration.
