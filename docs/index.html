Core Features

01

Focus Stats

Track your focus time, streak, and time saved with a beautiful circular progress indicator right on your dashboard.

02

Grayscale Mode

Reduce the visual dopamine hits of color. Grayscale mode makes your screen calm, less stimulating, and easier to put down.

03

Smart Notifications

Show notification dots. Filter distracting social media notifications. See numerical counts only for calls and SMS.

04

Modern Layouts

Choose between the classic grid or a modern List Layout. Full transparency control for tiles allows your wallpaper to shine through.

05

Backup & Restore

Export your entire launcher configuration and restore it anytime. Never lose your carefully crafted setup.

06

Dark Theme

A deep, true-black interface designed for OLED screens and nighttime usage. Easy on the eyes, saving battery.

07

Light Mode

A clean, high-contrast palette for daytime usage. Choose between 12-hour and 24-hour clock formats.

08

Dotz AI Assistant

Integrated Generative AI Assistant powered by Llama 3.1. Ask anything directly from your dashboard.

09

AI Summary

Intelligent notification categorization. See important messages and updates at a glance without clutter.

10

Detox Control Panel

Quick access to essential system toggles like WiFi, Bluetooth, and Torch, keeping you focused on the task at hand.

11

Accurate Weather

Real-time data from MET Norway with automatic location detection. Get the forecast for exactly where you are.

12

Universal Remapping

Every tile slot can be assigned to any app. Intelligent suggestions help you find the right tool for your workflow quickly.
