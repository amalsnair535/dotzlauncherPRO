01 — The Home Experience

Intentional by design.

A 12-tile layout across two pages — six apps at a time, nothing more. Every element earns its place on your screen.

  • Minimalist Grid: 6 tiles per page, high-intentionality layout designed to reduce clutter and unconscious browsing.
  • Extra Power Tiles: Optional third page with up to 6 additional customizable tiles for secondary tools.
  • Flexible Flow: Choose between traditional horizontal paging or a modern vertical scrolling flow.
  • Smart Remapping: Every tile is fully customizable with manual assignments and purpose-based suggestions.
  • Budgeted App Drawer: A vertical "All Apps" drawer limited to 5 opens per day to break mindless browsing habits.

02 — Multi-Mode Smart Header

Three modes,
one glance.

The home header adapts to what you need — quick controls, music, or focus data — all switchable on the fly.

  • Quick Toggles: Instant access to WiFi, Bluetooth, Silent Mode, and Data — no swipe-down needed.
  • Now Playing: Full music control with track info and progress tracking built into your home screen.
  • Focus Stats: Real-time unlock counts and your personalized Focus Score at a glance.
  • Live Weather: Real-time temperature and conditions powered by the privacy-first Open-Meteo API.
  • Hardware Clock: Minimalist clock with 12h/24h format support and dynamic date display.

03 — Fastlane Timeline PRO

Your day,
in sequence.

Swipe right from home to enter Fastlane — a PRO-exclusive chronological, text-driven timeline of everything that happened today. No icons, no noise.

  • Chronological Feed: Vertical timeline of daily notifications and app activity, newest first.
  • Quick Reply: Respond to messages directly from the timeline — zero context switching.
  • Music Integration: Current and recent media activity flows naturally into the timeline.
  • Text-Driven: Colorful app icons are removed. Pure information, zero visual noise.
  • Usage Tracking: See exactly how long each app was open with "Used for Xm" labels.
No IconsInline ReplyChronological

04 — Digital Detox & Mindfulness

Your phone works
for you.

Dotz tracks how you use your phone and gently nudges you toward healthier habits — without being preachy.

  • Focus Score: A dynamic score (0–100) tracking your phone intentionality based on unlocks and screen time.
  • Icon Dimming: "Exhausted" app icons fade to 20% opacity after 30 minutes of use today.
  • Mindful Check-in: Intentionality prompts when opening a distracting app for the 3rd time in a day.
  • Usage Badges: Real-time "minutes spent" counters on home tiles and in the app drawer.
  • Midnight Reset: All daily budgets and metrics reset automatically at 12:00 AM.

05 — Customization (PRO)

Your launcher,
your identity.

PRO unlocks the full depth of Dotz — adaptive themes, transparent overlays, scheduled grayscale, and multi-profile setups.

  • Home Screen Profiles: Create and switch between "Work" and "Home" setups with distinct app assignments.
  • Circadian Theming: UI colors shift dynamically from cool morning blues to warm evening ambers.
  • Transparent Mode: Blends the minimalist interface seamlessly with your system wallpaper.
  • Grayscale Scheduling: Automated night-time grayscale — turns on after 10 PM automatically.
  • Tile Transparency: Precision slider control over app tile background opacity.
  • Change Wallpaper: Set custom wallpapers directly from launcher settings.

06 — Privacy & Performance

Nothing leaves
your device.

No accounts. No cloud sync. No tracking. Dotz runs entirely on your device — your data stays yours, always.

  • 100% Local: No personal data, usage stats, or app lists ever leave your device.
  • No Cloud AI: No cloud trackers or invasive algorithms. Pure, fast, on-device utility.
  • Ultra-Lightweight: Optimized for zero lag and minimal battery consumption.
  • Open Source: Every line of code is public on GitHub — audit it yourself.
  • Indus Appstore: Available on India's own app store alongside GitHub releases.
